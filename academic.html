Syllabus Modules
MSJ 2251 / Digital Journalism Major

Journalism and Society

add

From the origins of the press to today's digital newsrooms, this course explores how journalism shapes and is shaped by society, covering news writing, media ideologies, production processes, source dynamics, audience behavior, ethics, and global news flows.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Journalism Terminologies
  • Reporting Basics
  • News Production Processes
  • Ethics and Professionalism
MSJ 3151 / Digital Journalism Major

News Sourcing and Gathering

add

A hands-on course in the craft of reporting, covering how journalists find stories, identify and interview sources, cover events, verify facts, use AI tools, and navigate ethical challenges in today's fast-moving news environment.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Source Identification & Mapping
  • Interviewing Techniques
  • Fact-Checking & Verification
  • Beat-Based Field Reporting
MSJ 4162 / Communication for Development Major

Environmental Communication

add

This course unpacks how environmental issues are communicated across media, politics, and culture, covering climate justice, advocacy campaigns, global conferences, environmental laws, green marketing, and the evolving role of journalism in the climate crisis.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Environmental Advocacy Campaigns
  • Environmental Journalism
  • Environmental Policies and Laws
  • Environment & Development
SSC 3254 / General Education

History of Bangladeshi Media

add

From the colonial-era Bengali press to today's social media landscape, this course traces the defining chapters of Bangladesh's media history, covering newspaper growth, radio, television, digital platforms, the blogosphere, and media legislation.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Colonial Press Legacy
  • Military-Era Media
  • Satellite TV Boom
  • Digital & Social Media
