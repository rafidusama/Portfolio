Interactive Syllabus Modules
MSJ 2251 / Digital Journalism Major

Journalism and Society

add

Serving as a conceptual immersion into the role of journalism in Bangladesh, this course analyzes its function as a catalyst for social, political, economic, and cultural change. Key areas of study include the role of a free press in a democratic society, principles of journalistic reporting, and the impact of digital media ecosystems.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Ethics & Fourth Estate
  • Public Sphere Theory
  • Platform Hegemony
  • Delta Media Landscapes
MSJ 3151 / Digital Journalism Major

News Sourcing and Gathering

add

This course equips students with core practices for producing high-quality news content: identifying authoritative sources, gathering empirical evidence, interviewing, and employing advanced verification methodologies. It combines a comprehensive overview of the media industry in Bangladesh with intensive hands-on fact-checking training.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Field Sourcing Tactics
  • OSINT Fact-Checking
  • Interview Methodologies
  • Verification Workflows
MSJ 4162 / Communication for Development Major

Environmental Communication

add

A major area of study within the Communication for Development (C4D) concentration, this course covers theoretical approaches, research literature, and practical applications for communicating environmental issues, delta ecology, and climate science within mass media, political, and organizational contexts.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Climate Communication
  • Ecology & Social Nature
  • Disaster Communication
  • Delta Advocacy Models
SSC 3254 / General Education

History of Bangladeshi Media

add

This course traces the historical evolution of media in Bangladesh from its colonial inception to the digital era. It covers the development of the press since 1947, the rise of state and private television, changing radio formats, the national film industry, and the contemporary impacts of citizen journalism.

Core Syllabus Themes:
  • Colonial Print Roots
  • 1971 Liberation Press
  • Satellite TV Boom
  • Citizen Media Era
