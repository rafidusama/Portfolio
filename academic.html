Syllabus Modules

MSJ 2251 / Digital Journalism Major

Journalism and Society

+

From the origins of the press to today's digital newsrooms, this course explores how journalism shapes and is shaped by society, covering news writing, media ideologies, production processes, source dynamics, audience behavior, ethics, and global news flows.

Core Syllabus Themes:

  • Journalism Terminologies
  • Reporting Basics
  • News Production Processes
  • Ethics and Professionalism
MSJ 2252 / Digital Journalism Major

Digital Audience

+

The advent of Information and Communication Technologies has reshaped how media organizations produce, distribute, and connect with audiences. This course examines the digital audience: who they are, how they behave, and why monitoring their engagement matters, covering audience theory, social psychology, and how community and consumption patterns converge across platforms and industries.

Core Syllabus Themes:

  • Audience Theory and Concepts
  • Audience Research Methods
  • Audience Analytics and Monitoring
  • Ethics and Engagement

Newsfluencer: The Intersection of Online Content Production and Platform-Based Journalism

Nirikkha Issue 254/Press Institute Bangladesh (PIB)

A Bangla translation and contextual adaptation of Edward Hurcombe's conceptual article "Conceptualising the Newsfluencer", published in Nirikkha (Issue 254) by the Press Institute Bangladesh. The article traces the rise of platformised news creators who operate according to the economic and cultural logics of online influencer culture.

Read the Paper ↗
Abstract

"Journalists and other news content creators are negotiating with participatory platforms and entrepreneurial business models. This conceptual article defines and traces the rise of “newsfluencers”: platformatised creators who operate according to the economic and cultural logics of online influencers to produce news content for participatory audiences. Newsfluencers encompass self-employed journalists as well as Substack writers, journalistic YouTubers and TikTokers, and Twitch streamers. This article explicates newsfluencers across four intersecting dimensions: platforms, business models, labour, and cultures. In doing so, the article demonstrates how the news industry, influencer and fandom cultures, platforms, and the creator economy are interconnecting with and transforming each other."
