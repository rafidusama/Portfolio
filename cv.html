Curriculum Vitae

Usama
Rafid

Academician and researcher focusing on journalism practices at the intersection of media business and emerging technologies. Teaches journalism theories and practices, blending academic insight with real newsroom experience. Brings more than six years of combined experience in newsrooms and research institutions. Holds Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Mass Communication and Journalism from the University of Dhaka, with multiple awards for academic performance.

Research Interests and Skills

Research Interests

  • Journalism Studies: Investigating newsroom dynamics, reporting precarity, editorial independence, and press autonomy in Bangladeshi and South Asian media environments.
  • Digital Journalism: Studying how platforms, AI tools, and online publishing reshape news production, newsroom practices, and journalistic work in Bangladesh.
  • News and Audience: Examining how audiences find, consume, trust, and pay for news in digital environments across Bangladesh.

Skills

  • Media Research
  • Data Driven Reporting
  • AI and Journalism
