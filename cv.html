Profile

Academician and researcher focusing on journalism practices at the intersection of media business and emerging technologies. Teaches journalism theories and practices, blending academic insight with real newsroom experience. Brings more than six years of combined experience in newsrooms and research institutions. Holds Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Mass Communication and Journalism from the University of Dhaka, with multiple awards for academic performance.
