Foundations

Knowing which agency to call, and how quickly to expect anything back, is the difference between a story that gets an official response in the piece and one that says "did not respond to a request for comment" because the request went to the wrong desk.

The Ministry Structure

MoEFCC

The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change is the lead policy ministry — parent to the Department of Environment and the Forest Department.

MoWR

The Ministry of Water Resources oversees water infrastructure policy — parent to BWDB and CEGIS.

MoDMR

The Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief oversees disaster response and relief coordination.

Jurisdiction Overlaps

The Same Issue, Multiple Agencies

A river pollution story can touch DoE (industrial effluent, permits), BWDB (water infrastructure, embankments), and the Ministry of Water Resources (policy) all at once, and each may point you elsewhere. Ask each agency directly what specifically falls under its jurisdiction, and document if you're passed back and forth — that pattern is itself worth noting in the story.

Typical Response Patterns

Government agencies in Bangladesh generally respond faster to formal written requests than informal phone calls, and multi-layer approval chains mean even a routine request can take time. These are general working patterns, not guarantees — always build in more time than feels necessary and follow up in writing.
