Foundations

Waiting for news to happen is a passive way to run a beat. A systematic prompt bank, tied to a real calendar of recurring events, produces story ideas on a schedule instead of by chance.

Prompt Categories

Anniversary

A disaster, policy, or finding's one-year (or five-year) mark — a natural hook for a follow-up.

Data Release

Scheduled government or research data publications, known in advance and easy to plan around.

Seasonal

Recurring conditions — dry season scarcity, monsoon flooding, cyclone risk windows.

Policy Deadline

A commitment, review, or negotiation deadline coming due — a chance to check on follow-through.
