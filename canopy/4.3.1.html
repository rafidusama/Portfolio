Module 4 · Ch.3 · 4.3.1

Foundations

Bangladesh has more official environmental data available than most reporters use, spread across a handful of portals with very different access rules — some genuinely open, some requiring a formal, fee-based request with a real wait attached.

The Official Portals

data.gov.bd

Bangladesh's national open data portal, with dedicated Environment and Agriculture categories — genuinely open, no request process required.

BMD Data Portal

The Bangladesh Meteorological Department's formal data request system — not open access, requires an application.

BBS Data Catalog

The Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics' national statistical data catalog, including a dedicated environment, climate, and disaster statistics program.

CCIKM Portal

Climate Change Knowledge Management portal, useful as a curated jumping-off point to both domestic and international climate data resources.

Known Limitations and Gaps

Open Isn't Always Current

Some of the most useful open datasets — like BMD's historical temperature, humidity, and rainfall records on data.gov.bd — cover a fixed historical window (1948 to 2014 in that specific dataset) rather than updating continuously. For more recent data, expect to file a formal request through BMD's own data portal.

BMD's formal data request process caps look-back requests to roughly three months at a time, takes a minimum of five working days to fulfill, and comes with a government-set fee — a real constraint to plan around if a story needs a fast turnaround on recent weather data.

"Budget for the wait. A data request that takes five working days minimum doesn't fit into a same-day story — plan ahead, or use what's already open."
