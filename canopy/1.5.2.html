Field Craft

Officials and institutional spokespeople are often trained, formally or informally, to protect the institution first. That's not necessarily dishonesty — it's the job. Your job is to ask in a way that still gets accountability-relevant material out of the exchange, even when the other side has every incentive to give you as little as possible.

Anticipating Deflection

The Reassurance

"We take this very seriously" — sounds responsive, commits to nothing specific or checkable.

The Redirect

Answers a nearby, easier question instead of the one you asked.

The Stall

"I'll have to check and get back to you" — often means never, unless you set a specific deadline.

The Buck-Pass

Directs you to another agency or department, who may direct you right back.

Follow-Up Techniques

Four Ways to Press Without Escalating

  • Narrow the question. If the answer is general, ask the same thing again with less room to generalize.
  • Convert to yes/no. A specific factual question is harder to deflect than an open one.
  • Set a concrete deadline. "I'll check and get back to you" becomes reportable if you ask "by when."
  • Cite the specific document or date. Naming the exact record you're asking about makes a general answer visibly non-responsive.

Documenting Refusals

"The agency declined to comment" is weak and vague. "The agency did not respond to specific questions about the 2022 warning or whether a formal inspection has occurred, saying only that the matter is under routine review" tells the reader exactly what was asked and exactly what wasn't answered — which is itself a reportable fact.

Navigating Access Barriers

Many Bangladeshi institutions route all comment through a single designated spokesperson, which can mean long delays or no response at all. When formal channels stall, a written RTI request creates a paper trail and a legal response deadline; showing up in person can sometimes get past a gatekeeper who ignores calls; and documenting every attempt — date, method, and outcome — protects you if the institution later claims they were never asked.
