Field Craft

Fire detection satellites don't just track wildfires — they're a genuinely useful tool for the seasonal, on-the-ground realities of brick kiln enforcement and agricultural burning, both real recurring beats in Bangladesh.

Near Real-Time Fire Detection in Practice

Brick Kiln Verification

A cluster of heat signatures over a known kiln zone during the seasonal ban period is a direct, checkable signal worth following up with a site visit.

Agricultural Burning

Post-harvest field burning shows up as widespread, scattered detections concentrated in specific weeks — useful for air-quality-cause stories tied to a specific season.

EM-DAT as a Historical Reference

Where NASA FIRMS gives you what's happening right now, EM-DAT gives you the historical baseline — how many comparable disasters a district or country has recorded, going back decades. Citing "this is the third major flood event recorded for this district since 2010" requires exactly this kind of historical reference, not just this week's news.

"A single fire detection is a data point. A pattern against a historical baseline is a story."
