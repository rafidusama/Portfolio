Field Craft

Industrial environmental stories almost always have a labor story running underneath them — the same effluent that pollutes a river is usually processed by workers with their own safety exposure. Treating the two as separate stories misses how directly connected they usually are.

Chattogram Ship-Breaking Yards

Ship-breaking at Chattogram's coastal yards combines serious environmental concerns — oil, heavy metals, and asbestos entering the coastal environment — with some of the most hazardous manual labor conditions in the country. A story on hull contamination and a story on worker injury rates are often the same reporting trip.

Tannery Relocation: Hazaribagh to Savar

The relocation of Dhaka's tanneries from Hazaribagh to a purpose-built site in Savar was meant to solve a decades-long effluent crisis by centralizing treatment. Whether the promised treatment capacity has actually kept pace with production volume is a recurring, checkable story — don't assume the relocation solved the problem just because it happened.

Access Challenges

Expect to Be Turned Away

Industrial site access is frequently denied, and a refusal itself is a reportable fact, not a dead end. Document exactly who denied access, when, and what reason was given — this becomes part of the story regardless of whether you ever get inside.
